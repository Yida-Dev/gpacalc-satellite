Understanding GPA Scales

Different countries and institutions use different grading scales:

  • 4.0 Scale (US/Canada) - Most common in North America. A=4.0, B=3.0, C=2.0, D=1.0
  • 5.0 Scale (Nigeria/Ghana) - Used in West African universities. A=5.0, B=4.0, C=3.0
  • 10.0 Scale (India) - Used in many Indian universities under CBCS system
  • Percentage (UK/Australia) - Often converted to First Class, 2:1, 2:2 classifications

Tips for Improving Your GPA

  • Use a GPA calculator at the start of each semester to set realistic targets
  • Focus on high-credit courses as they carry more weight in cumulative GPA
  • Attend office hours regularly to clarify concepts early
  • Form study groups for challenging subjects
  • Review past exams and assignments to understand grading patterns
